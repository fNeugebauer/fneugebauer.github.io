# Detailed Descriptions 

3. **Gros Topoi as Partially Lax Limits of Petit Topoi**
joint with Qi Zhu

We prove that the sheaf $\infty$-topos associated to a geometric site in the sense of Lurie can be written as a partially lax limit of smaller sheaf $\infty$-topoi. This is thus a formalization of Lurie's vision for fractured $\infty$-topoi to axiomatize the relation between gros and petit topoi. As a consequence, we realize the $\infty$-topos of $\kappa$-small condensed anima as a partially lax limit of sheaf $\infty$-topoi over extremally disconnected spaces, marked at the open embeddings. Moreover, we deduce a gros version of the étale exodromy theorem.

[arXiv:2609.35119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35119), [PDF](/files/Gros_Topoi_as_Partially_Lax_Limits_of_Petit_Topoi.pdf)

2. **Formal Group Laws and Stable Homotopy Theory over Compactly Metrizable Groups** 
Masters Thesis in Chromatic Equivariant Homotopy Theory

We extend the theory of complex-oriented cohomology theories to the setting of A-equivariant homotopy theory, where A is a compact metrizable abelian group. We identify the homotopy groups of the A-equivariant complex bordism spectrum with the A-equivariant Lazard ring, and we factor the chromatic character map via geometric fixed points through p-adic equivariant Borel–Lubin–Tate theory.

[PDF](/files/master_thesis_final.pdf)

1. **Algorithmic Computation of Reidemeister Torsion** 
Bachelors Thesis in Computational Algebraic Topology

We develop an algorithm that computes the Reidemeister Torsion of the geometric realization of a simplicial set. We compute the Reidemeister Torsions of the Poincare Homology 3-Sphere.
